01

وضعیت فعلی سایتWhere the site stands today

We pulled a complete copy of the existing site and everything in its media library before touching anything. Here is what that turned up.

گواهی SSL

سایت از کار نیفتاده است؛ فقط گواهی امنیتی آن معتبر نیست.

The server is running fine. In September a self-signed certificate was installed, so every browser now shows a full-page security warning before the site loads. A free Let's Encrypt certificate fixes this in about ten minutes, and it should be done this week regardless of the rebuild.

محتوا

آخرین مطلب سایت مربوط به اسفند ۱۳۹۹ است.

Eight pages and four posts in total, last updated March 2021. The Activity page is empty, and book prices are frozen at 2020 figures. Meanwhile the Instagram account has 6,277 followers and posts regularly — the audience is there, the website just stopped.

شماره‌ی ۸

ویژه‌نامه‌ی مرغ دریایی چاپ شده، اما هرگز در سایت اعلام نشد.

The cover file for issue 8 is dated October 2021. The homepage still announces it as forthcoming — five years later.

آرشیو

هر هشت جلد فصلنامه و ۱۷ جلد کتاب بازیابی شد.

112 MB of original artwork recovered, including the hand-drawn چارسو wordmark at 4500×4500. 29 files on the server are empty and unrecoverable, but nothing essential was lost.

ساختار پیشنهادیThe structure we propose

انتشارات پیام چارسو is the parent brand and the quarterly is one line within it, so the catalogue leads and the magazine sits beside it. The old site did the opposite, with the press squeezed onto a single page.

  • خانه/

  • صفحه‌ی هر شماره — جلد، فهرست مطالب، نویسندگان/magazine/issues/:n
  • اشتراک/magazine/subscribe

  • اخبار و رویدادها/news
  • درباره‌ی ما/about
  • تماس با ما/contact

The contributor index is the new idea. The back catalogue contains exclusive interviews with Peter Brook and with Eugenio Barba and Julia Varley, plus writing from محمد چرمشیر، ناهید طباطبایی، نسیم مرعشی and David Crystal in translation. Today those names sit as unlabelled text inside the old page builder. Indexed properly, they are a reason for theatre scholars anywhere to find this press.

03 — پرسش‌ها / questions

شش پرسش که پیش از شروع طراحی نیاز داریمSix answers we need before building

These are the only genuine blockers. Everything else we can decide and show you.

آیا انتشار فصلنامه ادامه دارد؟ آخرین شماره‌ی منتشرشده کدام است؟

Is the quarterly still publishing, and which issue is current?

Why it matters — issue 8 (ویژه‌نامه‌ی مرغ دریایی) was printed in autumn 2021. If there are issues 9 or 10 we have no record of them, and the archive would be wrong on the day it launches.

فروش آنلاین می‌خواهید، یا مانند گذشته از راه تماس و کتاب‌فروشی‌ها؟

Do you want to sell online, or keep the current model?

Why it matters — today the site says "call or email us" and links out to ایران‌کتاب، کتاب‌فروشی پیشگاه، پخش گسترش and نشر چشمه. Keeping that is perfectly respectable and much simpler. Adding an Iranian payment gateway is possible but meaningfully more work and more upkeep.

متن و فایل شماره‌های پیشین به‌صورت دیجیتال موجود است؟ (PDF یا این‌دیزاین)

Do you have the back issues digitally — PDFs or InDesign files?

Why it matters — this decides whether each issue page carries a real table of contents and named contributors, or just a cover image and a paragraph. It is the single biggest difference in how substantial the finished site feels.

فایل‌های اصلی جلد شماره‌های ۳، ۴، ۵ و ۸ در دسترس است؟

Can we get the original cover files for issues 3, 4, 5 and 8?

Why it matters — the copies on the server are too small to display. Issue 8 survives only at 157×209 pixels. See the table below.

میزبانی سایت در ایران باشد یا خارج از ایران؟ دسترسی به دامنه و هاست فعلی با چه کسی است؟

Should hosting stay inside Iran, and who controls the domain today?

Why it matters — a .ir domain on an Iranian server rules out several common hosting platforms. We need to know who holds the domain and hosting logins before anything can move.

آیا از دفتر شما می‌توان به‌راحتی به github.com دسترسی داشت؟

Can you reach github.com comfortably from your office?

Why it matters — you have told us you want to add books and issues yourselves, a few times a year. Our preferred setup gives you a simple editing panel with image upload and no software to maintain — but it signs in through GitHub. Please open github.com and tell us whether it loads normally. If it does not, we will use a different approach; either way the editing experience is the same.
