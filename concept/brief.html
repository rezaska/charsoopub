01

وضعیت فعلی سایتWhere the site stands today

We pulled a complete copy of the existing site and everything in its media library before touching anything. Here is what that turned up.

گواهی SSL

سایت از کار نیفتاده است؛ فقط گواهی امنیتی آن معتبر نیست.

The server is running fine. In September a self-signed certificate was installed, so every browser now shows a full-page security warning before the site loads. A free Let's Encrypt certificate fixes this in about ten minutes, and it should be done this week regardless of the rebuild.

محتوا

آخرین مطلب سایت مربوط به اسفند ۱۳۹۹ است.

Eight pages and four posts in total, last updated March 2021. The Activity page is empty, and book prices are frozen at 2020 figures. Meanwhile the Instagram account has 6,277 followers and posts regularly — the audience is there, the website just stopped.

شماره‌ی ۸

ویژه‌نامه‌ی مرغ دریایی چاپ شده، اما هرگز در سایت اعلام نشد.

The cover file for issue 8 is dated October 2021. The homepage still announces it as forthcoming — five years later.

کاتالوگ

کانال تلگرام نشان می‌دهد سایت دست‌کم یک سال از انتشارات عقب است.

Reading the channel turned up an entire series we had no record of (جیبی‌های چارسو), a translation of the 2025 International Booker winner, a podcast made with Qoqnoos, and three plays by Ahmad Aram named in his obituary. The site's distributor list was also wrong — it still named پخش گسترش and نشر چشمه from 2017, where your own pinned post says ققنوس. That is now corrected.

آرشیو

هر هشت جلد فصلنامه و ۱۷ جلد کتاب بازیابی شد.

112 MB of original artwork recovered, including the hand-drawn چارسو wordmark at 4500×4500. 29 files on the server are empty and unrecoverable, but nothing essential was lost.

ساختار پیشنهادیThe structure we propose

انتشارات پیام چارسو is the parent brand and the quarterly is one line within it, so the catalogue leads and the magazine sits beside it. The old site did the opposite, with the press squeezed onto a single page.

  • خانه/
  • کتاب‌ها cataloguePRIMARY/books
  • صفحه‌ی هر کتاب/books/:slug
  • فصلنامه the quarterly/magazine
  • آرشیو هشت شماره/magazine
  • صفحه‌ی هر شماره — جلد، فهرست مطالب، نویسندگان/magazine/:n
  • نویسندگان و مترجمانپرسش ۶/people
  • اخبار و رویدادها/news
  • درباره‌ی ما/about
  • تماس با ما/contact
  • English/en

The contributor index is the new idea. The back catalogue contains exclusive interviews with Peter Brook and with Eugenio Barba and Julia Varley, plus writing from محمد چرمشیر، ناهید طباطبایی، نسیم مرعشی and David Crystal in translation. Today those names sit as unlabelled text inside the old page builder. Indexed properly, they are a reason for theatre scholars anywhere to find this press.

03 — پرسش‌ها / questions

پرسش‌هایی که هنوز پاسخ نگرفته‌اندWhat we still need from you

Four of the original six are answered: the quarterly is still publishing, there will be no online sales, hosting is settled, and the CMS route works. Reading the Telegram channel then raised several new ones. These are what remain.

آخرین شماره‌ی منتشرشده‌ی فصلنامه کدام است؟

Which issue of the quarterly is current?

Why it matters — we have through issue 8 (مرغ دریایی، پاییز ۱۴۰۰). If there is a 9 or a 10 the archive is wrong on the day it launches, and the homepage will feature the wrong cover.

آیا «برای یک بار هم که شده، زن باش» منتشر شده است؟

Is the Banu Mushtaq translation out yet?

Why it matters — announced on the channel in آذر ۱۴۰۴ and never confirmed since — eleven months of «به‌زودی». The original won the International Booker Prize 2025, which makes it the most newsworthy title in the catalogue. It is on the site now marked forthcoming; one word from you moves it. Also: is the cover title «چراغ دل» or «برای یک بار هم که شده زن باش»? The channel uses both.

نام سه نمایشنامه‌ی احمد آرام که چارسو منتشر کرده چیست؟

What are the titles of the three Ahmad Aram plays?

Why it matters — the channel's obituary says «انتشارات چارسو افتخار داشت از او سه نمایشنامه منتشر کند», but none of the 23 titles on the site are his. He died in مهر, so people are looking for his work right now and finding nothing on his own publisher's site.

رادیو چارسو منتشر شده است؟

Has Radio Charsoo launched?

Why it matters — announced دی ۱۴۰۴ with انتشارات ققنوس and استودیوی رادیو پنجره. This is the only question that changes the site's structure rather than its content — a podcast is neither a book nor an issue and would need a section of its own.

از سال ۱۴۰۰ تا ۱۴۰۴ چه کتاب‌هایی منتشر شده است؟

What was published between 2021 and 2025?

Why it matters — the biggest blind spot. The old website stopped in اسفند ۱۳۹۹ and the Telegram channel only goes back to آبان ۱۴۰۴. Four years in between with no record at all. A simple list of titles is enough to start.

کدام نویسندگان و مترجمان با چارسو همکاری داشته‌اند؟

Who has written for and translated for Charsoo?

Why it matters — from the back issues we can see Peter Brook, Eugenio Barba and Julia Varley interviewed, and writing by محمد چرمشیر، ناهید طباطبایی، نسیم مرعشی، رضا آشفته، هلن اولیایی‌نیا and others in translation. We would like to list contributors properly rather than leave those names buried inside old page text — a complete, confirmed list from you would be far better than what we can piece together.

دسترسی به دامنه — چه کسی تنظیمات DNS را تغییر می‌دهد؟

Who actually makes DNS changes for the domain?

Why it matters — charsoo-mag.ir is registered through IRNIC and often managed via a reseller panel rather than directly. This is the only thing blocking launch. Everything else can be added after the site is live. Note it may not be whoever set up the original hosting.

آیا شماره‌ی ۰۲۱۲۲۷۸۰۸۱۰ هنوز فعال است؟

Is the landline still in service after the move?

Why it matters — it was tied to the old ونک office and landlines rarely survive a move. It currently appears on every page of the site. The two mobile numbers from the channel will have moved with you.

کدام حساب اینستاگرام فعال است؟

Which Instagram account is the live one?

Why it matters — the old site linked @payamcharsoo and the new material uses @charsoopub. Both still exist. If the first is dormant it should be retired or pointed at the other, so nobody lands on a stale profile.

فهرست مطالب شماره‌ها
Tables of contents for all eight issues — a PDF, a Word file, or photographs of the printed contents pages. This decides whether each issue page carries a real index or just a cover.
قیمت‌های به‌روز
Current prices for everything in print. The figures on the site are from 2020 and two of the newest titles have no price at all. Also which titles are out of stock.
