The system is already in print

Eight issues of فصلنامه پیام چارسو share one construction: full-bleed artwork, a Persian numeral in a boxed corner, and a spot-colour band carrying the calligraphic wordmark. The band colour is the only thing that changes. That is the design system — recovered from the print files, not invented here.

    04 — Typography & RTL

    Set in Persian first

    The old site ran Tahoma defaults, mixed ۱۲۳ with 123 inside single sentences, and shipped a Gregorian calendar widget labelled in Farsi. All three are typographic tells that the site isn't maintained.

    ویژه‌نامه‌ی مرغ دریایی
    Vazirmatn 900 · headingsvariable, self-hosted

    Display weight for issue and book titles. Vazirmatn is the strongest open-source Persian family — properly built, variable, and it carries Latin glyphs so bilingual lines stay on one face.

    فصلنامه‌ی تخصصی تئاتر و سینمای پیام چارسو در زمستان ۱۳۹۵ موفق به اخذ پروانه‌ی انتشار به شماره‌ی ۷۸۸۷۵ با گستره‌ی سراسری به دو زبان فارسی و انگلیسی گردید.
    Vazirmatn 300 · running textline-height 1.95

    Persian needs more leading than Latin — descenders and diacritics collide at 1.6. Set body copy at 1.9–2.0 and keep measure near 60 characters.

    ۱ ۲ ۳ ۴ ۵ ۶ ۷ ۸ ۹ ۰
    Persian numerals · tabular۳۸٬۰۰۰ تومان · ۱۳۹۵

    Persian numerals everywhere, with the Arabic thousands separator ٬ for prices. Dates in the Shamsi calendar. One system, no mixing — this is the detail that reads as care.

    05 — Principles

    Four rules this direction holds to

    The wordmark is untouched

    The hand-drawn calligraphic چارسو is the best asset in the archive — 4500×4500, brush-drawn, unmistakable. It carries every cover and sits as a colophon on every book. Keep it; don't redraw it.

    Colour comes from the issue

    No house palette competing with the covers. The page borrows the accent of whatever it's showing, and otherwise stays black, white and paper. One spot colour at a time.

    Covers are the interface

    هشت جلد، هشت رنگ

    The artwork is strong enough to navigate by. Archive, hero and picker are all cover-led — no thumbnails cropped to uniform squares, which would destroy the band.

    Built RTL, not flipped

    Logical properties throughout, dir="rtl" on the document. Latin runs are isolated so English names inside Persian sentences don't reorder the line.

    06 — Blocking

    Four covers can't be used at this size

    Everything above is built from files recovered off the live server. Four of the eight are below usable web resolution — we need the originals from whoever set the covers before this direction can ship.
